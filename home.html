ABOUT US

PROSION Therapeutics is a biotech company with a proprietary technology combining the advantages of small molecules and biologics to unlock undruggable disease drivers.

Starting off in oncology and adjacent pathologies, we utilize our cutting-edge technology for a variety of other therapeutic areas with the mission to impact the lives of patients for the better.

Meet ProMs

ProMs are the first and currently only mimetics of the Polyproline-II-Helix, a specific helix structure that is recognized by a highly abundant yet so far undruggable class of protein targets.
